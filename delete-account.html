Delete your account and data

App: Jothidam Today: Tamil Jathagam (Digi Product Factory)
Last updated: 19 November 2026

Jothidam Today has no email or password account. It uses an anonymous, random ID created on your phone. Deleting your account removes the data tied to that ID.

How to delete

  1. Open Jothidam Today and go to Settings (the gear icon on the Today screen).
  2. Choose Delete account and all data, press and hold the delete button, then tap Delete everything in the confirmation that follows.

The app asks our server to delete your data first, and only then clears the data stored on your phone.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account.
  • Your free-question and credit balance and credit history.
  • Your AI usage records and any reports you sent.
  • All data stored on your phone by the app: profiles, birth details, settings and saved questions and answers.

Credits you bought are lost when the account is deleted and cannot be restored.

What we keep

Purchase records (product, credits, time, Google Play order number and a hash of the purchase token) with your app ID removed, kept for accounting, refunds and to prevent a Google Play purchase from being counted twice. Analytics and crash data held by Google Firebase follow Firebase's retention settings.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

If you have already uninstalled the app

The anonymous ID exists only inside the installed app, so we cannot match it to you from outside. Reinstalling creates a new, empty ID. If you need help, write to jothidamtoday.help@gmail.com and describe the problem.
