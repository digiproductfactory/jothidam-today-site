In short: your birth details and family profiles stay on your phone. When you ask the AI astrologer a question, the question and some calculated chart values are sent to our server and to Google's Gemini service to write the answer. There is no sign-up, email or phone number.

1. What the app does

Jothidam Today shows the daily Tamil Panchangam, a calendar, Rasi Palan, Thirumana Porutham, a Jathagam chart, and an AI astrologer that explains calculated chart facts in simple language. The AI answers are for guidance and entertainment only.

2. Information we handle

Stays on your phone. Names, birth dates, birth times, birth places, family profiles, the city you choose and your theme setting. We do not send these to our servers. The app calculates the Panchangam, charts and matches on the phone itself. (Your language setting is sent with each AI question, as described below.)

Saved questions and answers are stored only on your phone, and are deleted when you clear them, delete your account or uninstall the app.

Sent when you ask the AI a question.

  • Your question text (up to 500 characters) and up to a few earlier messages of the same chat.
  • Calculated chart facts for the selected profile: the day's Panchangam (including the date, your local time and the sunrise and sunset times for your chosen city), the profile's relation to you (for example "mother"), rasi, nakshatram, lagnam, the positions of the nine planets, the current dasha and bhukti with their dates, and Porutham results if a spouse is saved. We do not send your name, birth date, birth time or city name, but planet positions, dasha dates and sunrise times are derived from them and could indicate an approximate birth date or area.
  • Your chosen language.

This is processed by our server (Google Cloud Functions, Mumbai region) and by Google's Gemini API to produce the answer. Please do not type personal details, such as your name, phone number or address, into your question.

Google processes your question and the chart facts to write the answer. Under Google's Gemini API terms for paid services, Google does not use them to train or improve its models; Google may keep them for a limited period to detect abuse. We do not store your question or the answer on our server.

Anonymous account. The app signs you in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID. We do not ask for or store your email address or phone number.

Credits and purchases. We keep your free-question and credit balance and a record of credit changes. When you buy credits, payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or UPI details. We store the product bought, the credits granted, the time, the Google Play order number and a fingerprint (hash) of the Google Play purchase token, so the same purchase cannot be counted twice and so we can handle refunds and support requests.

Usage records. For each AI question we keep the time, language, token counts, status and estimated cost, to enforce limits, prevent abuse and manage costs. If you tap Report on an AI answer, we receive the report: the text of that answer, the reason you chose and your anonymous ID. Your question is not included.

Analytics and crash reports. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used: app events (such as screens viewed, questions asked, purchases and rewarded ads, with no question text, birth details or names), an app-instance identifier, your device's advertising ID, device model, Android version and language, and an approximate location (country or region) that Google derives from your IP address. We also use Firebase Crashlytics (crash traces and basic device information such as model and Android version).

Ads. Ads appear only when you choose to watch a short rewarded video for an extra question. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your ad consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. The ads come from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings.

Notifications. The optional 6 AM notification is created on your phone and sends nothing to us.

3. Who receives data

We use Google services to run the app: Firebase (authentication, database, server functions, App Check with Google Play Integrity to block fake requests, analytics, crash reports), the Gemini API (AI answers), Google Play (payments), and AdMob with Google's consent tool (ads). These services process data on our behalf or, for AdMob, under Google's own terms. We do not sell your data.

4. How long we keep it

Account data, credit records, usage records and reports are kept until you delete your account in the app. After deletion we keep only the purchase records (product, credits, time, Google Play order number and a hash of the purchase token), with your app ID removed, for accounting, refunds and to prevent a purchase being counted twice. If you uninstall the app without deleting your account first, the data linked to its anonymous ID stays on our server, because we cannot tell which ID was yours. We do not yet delete inactive IDs automatically. Our server keeps technical logs (time, status, response time and error messages, without your question, answer or ID) for up to 30 days. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google under its Firebase retention settings.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete account in the app. See the account deletion page.
  • Ads: reset or opt out of personalisation in Android settings.
  • Notifications: switch them off in the app or in Android settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. AI answers and astrology

Astrological content is traditional guidance, not a prediction of facts. AI answers can be wrong. Do not use them as medical, legal or financial advice.

8. Security

Data moves over encrypted connections, and our database allows access only through our server functions. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, where the change matters, tell you in the app.
